Fathom — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-02

Fathom is a personal notes app. This policy explains what data it handles.

What Fathom stores

All notes you create are stored only on your device, inside the app's local storage. Nothing you type into Fathom — note titles, details, due dates, tags — is sent anywhere. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, and no cloud sync.

What Fathom does NOT do

  • Fathom itself collects, transmits, or shares nothing with the developer or any third party.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs are included for the app's own purposes.
  • No advertising is shown, and no ad SDKs are included.

The one exception: the optional tip jar (see below) uses Google Play Billing, which requires internet access and communicates with Google's own servers to process purchases — this is Google's data handling, governed by Google's privacy policy, not data Fathom collects. Nothing else in the app makes any network call.

Permissions Fathom requests, and why

  • Notifications — to remind you when a note is due. Reminder checks run entirely on your device.

Sharing and calendar

Fathom's "Share" and "Add to calendar" buttons only run when you tap them. They hand a note's title, date, and details to the app you choose (a messaging app, your calendar, etc.) through Android's standard share/insert screens. What that app does with the text is governed by that app's own privacy policy; Fathom itself sends nothing anywhere, and does not read your calendar.

Backups you create

If you use the app's Export feature, a JSON backup file is written to a location you choose on your own device (via Android's standard file picker) — this is entirely under your control and never uploaded anywhere by the app.

Optional tip jar

Fathom includes an optional, entirely voluntary "Support Fathom" tip jar in Settings. Tips are processed as one-time in-app purchases through Google Play Billing. Fathom never sees or stores your payment details — that information is handled entirely by Google Play, under Google's own privacy policy. Fathom only learns that a purchase succeeded, not any payment information.

Changes to this policy

If Fathom's data handling ever changes, this page will be updated accordingly.
